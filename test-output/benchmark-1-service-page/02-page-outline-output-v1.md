PART 6: SCHEMA STRATEGY
Schema 1: LocalBusiness
Why it applies: This is a local service business in Las Vegas. LocalBusiness schema is the highest-priority schema on the page.
Required fields: name, address (streetAddress, city, state, postalCode), telephone, url, openingHours, areaServed
Optional enhancements: priceRange, sameAs (Google Business Profile URL), image, logo
Implementation: JSON-LD in page <head>. Do not duplicate in visible content.
Schema 2: Service
Why it applies: The page targets a specific service (furniture and appliance removal).
Required fields: serviceType, provider (linked to LocalBusiness), areaServed, description
Optional enhancements: offers (if pricing is published)
Implementation: JSON-LD, nested inside or alongside LocalBusiness block.
Schema 3: FAQPage
Why it applies: 10-question FAQ section directly targets People Also Ask and featured snippet placement. FAQPage schema increases eligibility for FAQ rich results in Google and Bing.
Required fields: mainEntity array — each Question with acceptedAnswer
Implementation: JSON-LD in page <head> or just before </body>. Each FAQ question and answer must match the visible on-page content exactly.
Risk: Do not add FAQPage schema if the FAQ section is not visible on the page.
Schema 4: BreadcrumbList
Why it applies: Establishes page hierarchy in SERP. Supports sitelinks and navigation signals for Google and Bing.
Required fields: itemListElement with position, name, item (URL) for each breadcrumb level
Example: Home → Services → Furniture & Appliance Removal
Implementation: JSON-LD, consistent with the visible breadcrumb navigation component.
