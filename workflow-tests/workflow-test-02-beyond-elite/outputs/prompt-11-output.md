## 5. SERP Intent Summary

Primary intent: Transactional / local service

The searcher typing "furniture removal Las Vegas" or "appliance removal Las Vegas"
has an immediate removal need. They are not researching the topic. They are
provider-shopping and want to hire someone now or get a quote today.

Secondary intent: Informational / trust validation

A portion of searchers want to confirm what items are accepted, how the process
works, whether the company is responsible with disposal, and what the cost
structure looks like before contacting anyone. This secondary intent must be
satisfied on the same page to prevent bouncing to an informational competitor.

User awareness stage: Problem-aware, solution-aware, provider-shopping.
The searcher knows they need junk removal. They are evaluating local providers.

Searcher primary questions (must be answered on this page):
- What does this service cover? (furniture types, appliance types)
- Do you serve my area of Las Vegas?
- How do I get a quote?
- Are you available soon?
- Can I trust this company?
- What happens to items after removal?
- Is pricing by item or by load?

AI assistant intent layer: AI assistants responding to "who removes furniture
in Las Vegas" or "best appliance removal Las Vegas" will look for pages that
directly answer the what, where, how, and how much questions with structured,
citable content. This page must satisfy both human searchers and AI citation
needs simultaneously.
