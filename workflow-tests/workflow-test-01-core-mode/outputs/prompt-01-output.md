14. Keyword Risks and Constraints

Same-day availability keywords — Do not use in headlines, H1, or meta title until client confirms same-day service. Use in FAQ only with a conditional flag.
Pricing keywords — Do not include specific dollar amounts in any on-page copy until client confirms pricing. The keyword opportunity is valid; the claim is not yet safe.
Service area long-tail keywords — Using city names like Henderson or Summerlin in copy or schema without client confirmation creates a geographic service claim risk. Carry forward.
Exact-match overuse risk — furniture removal Las Vegas appears across primary, secondary, and long-tail. Use natural variation (furniture hauling, large item removal, furniture pickup) in body copy to avoid stuffing signals.
Near-me keyword — High value for voice and local, but renders poorly as exact-match text in page copy. Use contextually, not as a literal phrase.
Duplicate content risk — If Pit Stop Junk Removal has a general junk removal page, the furniture/appliance page must be clearly differentiated with specific item lists, FAQ content, and schema.
