Part 1: SERP Intent Analysis
Primary Search Intent: Transactional / local service
The searcher wants to hire someone now or get a quote for furniture and/or appliance removal in Las Vegas. They are not researching the topic — they have an immediate need.
Secondary Search Intent: Informational / trust validation
Some searchers will want to confirm what is included (haul-away vs. disassembly), what items are accepted, whether the service is responsible (recycling, donation), and what the cost structure is before calling.
User Awareness Stage: Problem-aware, solution-aware, provider-shopping. The searcher knows what they need; they are evaluating providers.
Searcher Problem: They have furniture or appliances they cannot move themselves and need a reliable, affordable, local service to remove them quickly.
Searcher Desired Outcome: A confirmed quote or booked pickup with a trusted local company that is clearly available in Las Vegas.
What the page must answer quickly:

What does this service cover?
Do you serve my area of Las Vegas?
How do I get a price / quote?
Are you available soon?
Can I trust this company?

What would cause the user to bounce:

No clear price signal or quote path visible above the fold
No local Las Vegas signal in the first visible content
No trust signals (reviews, years in business, credentials)
Generic content that does not differentiate the business
Slow page load or poor mobile experience
